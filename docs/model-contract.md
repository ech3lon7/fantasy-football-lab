## Recorded values and projections

Use league-scored completed played games, including zero, negative and injury-shortened scores. Preserve nonparticipation/byes separately. Reconcile prepared actual aggregates with verified history. Invalid history makes the affected player unavailable. Season ESPN projected PPG is independent of current-week ESPN points; zero projections with no supporting game/point records remain unavailable.

## Adaptive values

Observed PPG uses the imported recent weight. Actual weight is games/(games+prior games). Expected PPG blends observed scoring with usable season projected PPG. Consistency loss is capped, uses sample SD and applies only after the configured minimum games with positive Expected PPG. Value PPG subtracts the loss. No history uses usable projections; missing projections require at least four actual games for observed-only value.

## Replacements and surplus

Average the top three usable active free agents at each position, excluding unusable history and players without remaining games. Sort Value PPG descending and PlayerID numerically ascending. One/two players make the baseline conditional; none makes it unavailable. Current byes do not remove season candidates.

VORP = (Value PPG − baseline) × available remaining games. Preserve negative VORP. Index = 100 × positive VORP / maximum positive VORP; missing remains null, while usable values with no positive peak return zero. Keep real-snapshot baselines fixed across hypothetical trades.

## Lineups, trades and tie rules

Port the supported workbook’s QB/RB/WR/TE, FLEX, K and D/ST selection order. Select by Value PPG descending and numeric PlayerID ascending. IR assignment preserves the imported rules and existing eligible IR slots. Confirmed current absences and byes are excluded; downside scenarios exclude all uncertain players.

Drop candidates must be droppable and reduce the current roster conflict. Minimize horizon lineup-value loss; ties use lower surplus index then PlayerID. Fill candidates maximize incremental horizon value; ties use higher surplus index then PlayerID. Recompute after each selection and preserve team processing order. Display dropped IDs in the source roster order, matching Excel’s output.

Grades compare incoming and outgoing Positive VORP without premiums. Package requirements separately apply the premium once for unequal sizes. Drop replacement costs belong only to the fantasy-points requirement. Trade-only, waiver-only and combined lineup results remain distinct. Legacy coverage Expected scoring uses the same starters chosen by Value PPG.

Trade discovery distinguishes marked-starter impact from roster potential. Marked starters retain their assigned roles: an existing reserve may fill only a departing starter's role, and incoming players may improve distinct roles. Surviving starters cannot receive unrelated reserve upgrades. A bench-only departure has zero direct impact. Roster potential independently selects full-roster lineups by Expected PPG both before and after the trade, using identical schedules, availability, IR rules and required drops; speculative waiver additions are excluded. Value PPG is reported for those same Expected-PPG lineups. Search and saved current summaries use roster-potential gain for both teams for filters and ranking, while exposing marked-starter impact and named weekly changes separately. Primary weekly gain is the total expected gain divided by every remaining fantasy week, including bye weeks, rather than a player’s PPG. The impact version is `roster-potential-primary-2`; older original summaries retain their original method. Saved current results recalculate; original summaries retain their scoring-method version. Legacy Value-PPG coverage calculations remain unchanged.

Position grades compare modeled starter value per slot against the imported fantasy-league median. Grade bands and the reserve cutoff are imported settings. FLEX is assigned before useful reserves; depth never boosts the starter grade.

## Provenance and states

Results are keyed by dataset content hash, settings and model version. Weights, replacement pools, premiums, grade bands and gem thresholds are provisional assumptions.

Each step has independent 1–5 outgoing/incoming ranges and optional opponent-team scope. The same counterparty may appear twice. Unselected original players remain protected from offers and required drops; newly acquired retained players may be offered in step two, but are not mandatory. Player forecasts, Value PPG, indices and snapshot replacement pools remain fixed. Marked-starter contexts carry exact weekly roles through step one; explicit subsequent roster edits/undo invalidate those frozen weekly overrides.

Discovery runs in a separate cancellable worker. Standard allows up to 24,000 unique first-stage candidate scans and 800 full first-stage evaluations; Deeper allows 120,000 and 2,400. No eligible scored first trade is discarded. Within each mode a deterministic deferred queue keeps the best representative of each package size first, followed by distinct acquired positional mixes/counterparty teams, then distinct acquired-player packages, and finally all remaining alternatives. Safe/speculative queues alternate when Both is selected. Fit matches, immediate gain, incoming index and deterministic package ties rank representatives and alternatives. This is an exploration order, never an acceptance bonus or a promise of finding the globally best bridge.

First-stage limits remain explicit and can leave uninspected first trades; search never claims exhaustive coverage in that case. Small selected universes exhaust when their complete work fits the shared allowances, with boundary peeks distinguishing exact exhaustion from a budget stop.

Results rank final gain, fit matches for equal gain, lower intermediate loss, then summed opponent gain. Keep at most the best 30 discovered chains while reporting all qualifying discoveries. `resultCapped` describes only that display limit; `limited` describes only incomplete search coverage. Diagnostics identify exhausted/unfinished first-stage streams, per-stage scans/evaluations, completed/unfinished follow-up branches, deferred first trades, remaining allowances and exact stage/budget stop reasons. No threshold, recipient concession, required-drop rule, player forecast or valuation changes to improve discovery.

Live-chain recipes explicitly retain live scheduled availability; ordinary hypothetical scenarios keep their existing bye/temporary-absence policy. ### Bounded chain-search coverage

Offer-size streams are interleaved. Every outgoing offer/opponent gets a separate exact incoming-size stream, with incoming players ordered by distance from the premium-aware comparable index per requested player rather than always starting at the opponent's highest Expected PPG. One traversal orders offered players by descending surplus index and prioritizes outgoing breadth; the other orders them by descending Expected PPG and tries an early second return. PlayerID breaks ties only. This avoids spending the bounded prefix on packages repeatedly anchored to low-ID filler, while preserving both high-surplus and high-scoring offers. They run round-robin with canonical draft deduplication before expensive evaluation; neither order removes a candidate from the unbounded universe. The first-stage and shared follow-up budgets remain explicit and may still omit valid chains; scored eligible first trades remain deferred rather than being removed by a permanent shortlist.

Provable recipient-concession failures are rejected before full scoring without changing either fairness or price predicates. Debug stats distinguish those rejections, full evaluations by first/follow-up package size, and the best final gain among inspected continuous chains passing every other check. If that best gain is below the requested final minimum, the UI displays both numbers separately without presenting it as a qualifying result or a global maximum. No filter is silently relaxed to produce a result.

## Surface-stat trade appeal

The opt-in `surface` search goal searches automatic index matching and 1–3 player returns, retains protected/tradable players, required targets and selected opponents, and defaults to your modeled gain >= +1 point per remaining week. It does not require their modeled scoring to improve. Select actual season average, the imported ESPN weekly projection, or Both (each must independently improve); the default apparent-gain threshold is zero but still requires strictly positive gains. Positive thresholds are inclusive without rounding. The preset ranks your model gain first. These are discovery preferences, not historically validated market-price or acceptance estimates.

Surface evidence evaluates the recipient's legal before/after roster after the normal evaluator's required drops, with no waiver additions or double-counted bench points. Both comparisons use the selected Best available / Marked starters basis. The season-average row is a scoring-rate comparison using recorded Actual PPG, neutral to known byes and temporary absences while excluding actual IR. The ESPN row is only the imported scoring period's matchup projection, with real scheduled availability even when the main model uses hypothetical bye-free scoring. Never substitute season projections or blended EPPG for the weekly source. Each source remains separate, including its before/after starters, points, role vacancies and null/missing status. Missing eligible source data makes that comparison unavailable, not zero. Recorded zero and negative values remain numeric.

The main model, its forecasts, replacement baselines, surplus indices, price-test formulas and lineup gains are unchanged by surface discovery; the separate chain presets control their own price-test requirement. Surface views are cached analysis-only copies with source values substituted solely for lineup scoring. The new discovery filter additionally requires your positive modeled gain and a legal modeled trade. Source timestamps and imported week labels remain visible; surface appeal never claims live data or likelihood of acceptance.
